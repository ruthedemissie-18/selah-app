The Meaning

A sacred pause.

A moment to stop, breathe,

and reflect on what is true.

The Fish

The ichthys.

An ancient symbol believers

once used to recognize

one another in secret —

a quiet sign of faith and belonging.

The Word

Scripture has always called us

toward each other, not away.

Let us spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together, but encouraging each other as we see the day approaching.

— Hebrews 10:24-25
